# PRD: Soundcheck v3 — The full Assistant

Terms in **bold** are defined in [`CONTEXT.md`](../../CONTEXT.md). This replaces the roadmap-level v3 doc now that [v2](v2.md) has shipped.

## Problem

v2 made every feature **Project** data, but the **Assistant** still only has the MVP's tools. It can read **Automation**, **Buses**, **Sends** and **Tempo Changes** in its context but not change them. It sees how many notes a **Pattern Clip** has but not which, so it can only rewrite a Clip from scratch. It can't copy anything, so "duplicate the chorus" is out of reach. Every **Request** starts from nothing, so "now make it darker" means nothing. The whole **Project** is sent on every turn, so a real song is expensive. It runs out of turns on a **Request** that needs several analyses (#79), and its clipping fix leaves no true-peak headroom (#80).

## Goal

The **Assistant** can do anything a musician can do by hand, in a back-and-forth **Conversation**, with whichever **Provider** the musician prefers, and it can check its own work by listening.

## Who it's for

The author first, making real songs with the **Assistant**. Then musicians on a **Local** model who need it to work offline, where a smaller model is less reliable.

## Already done

- A choice of **Provider**, model, version and effort: Claude, OpenAI, Google Gemini and Local (Ollama / llama.cpp), each with its own settings in the key store (#84, which covered #62 and the provider half of #63).
- Spectrogram images in **Audio Analysis**, opt-in per call (#26).
- **Plugin** tools: `set_instrument`, `set_instrument_settings` and `add_effect` take `plugin:<id>` (#54, #55).

## Architecture constraints

- Everything in v2's constraints still holds. The **Assistant** changes the **Project** only through the same commands the UI uses, and each **Request** is one undo step (`history.beginGroup` in `runRequest`).
- Every **Provider** implements the existing `StartConversation` / `Conversation` seam in `app/src/assistant/assistant.ts`. A feature a **Provider** lacks is declared in the catalogue, not discovered by failing. The abstraction must not drag every **Provider** down to the weakest one: what a model can do (tool use, images, audio input, several tool calls per turn) is a declared capability, and a feature is used wherever it is declared.
- No test calls a real model. Tests script the model's replies (`scripted(...)`) or stub `fetch`. The one real-model check (`app/scripts/real-model-check.ts`) is manual and spends money.
- The **Assistant** never sees a raw audio file unless the **Provider** accepts audio and the musician's setting allows it. **Audio Analysis** numbers work everywhere.
- A new **Project** field (a **Section**, a **Reference Track**) bumps the schema and ships a migration, as in v2.

## User stories

### The full toolset

1. As a musician, I can ask the **Assistant** to change specific notes: "make the last two notes of the bassline longer", "move the hi-hats up an octave", "quantise the melody to 1/16", "soften the ghost notes".
2. As a musician, I can ask it to add a **Bus**, route Tracks to it, add a **Send** and set its level, and change a **Bus's** volume, pan, mute and solo.
3. As a musician, I can ask it to draw **Automation**: "fade the pad in over the first four bars", "open the filter through the build-up".
4. As a musician, I can ask it to add or move a **Tempo Change** or a time-signature change.
5. As a musician, I can ask it to save the current sound as a User **Preset**, load a saved **Kit**, or save the Drum Sampler's **Pads** as a **Kit**.
6. As a musician, I can ask it to put a sample from my library or the **Project** onto an **Audio Track**, and to trim or move an **Audio Clip**.

### Arrangement

7. As a musician, I can name the parts of my song (intro, verse, chorus) as **Sections** on the ruler, by hand or by asking.
8. As a musician, I can ask the **Assistant** to duplicate a **Section**, insert or remove bars, and move a **Section** elsewhere: "repeat the chorus", "swap the second verse and the bridge".
9. As a musician, I can ask for a build-up into a **Section**, and get notes, **Automation** and **Effects** that do it, as one undo step.

### Conversations

10. As a musician, I can follow up on a **Request** ("now make it darker", "undo the reverb but keep the rest") and the **Assistant** knows what it and I just did.
11. As a musician, I can see the **Conversation** so far, and start a new one.

### Models

12. As a musician on a **Local** model, I get an **Assistant** that stays reliable: fewer tools, and changes offered as a **Suggestion** for me to apply rather than applied straight away.
13. As a musician on a model that accepts audio, the **Assistant** can listen to the rendered audio itself as well as read its **Audio Analysis**.

### Listening

14. As a musician, I can ask "did that fix it?" and the **Assistant** compares the sound before and after its changes.
15. As a musician, I can add a **Reference Track** and ask the **Assistant** to compare my mix with it.
16. As a musician, when I ask it to fix clipping, the mix ends up below a true-peak ceiling, and the loud Track is the one that comes down.

## Requirements

### Context and tools

- **A summary first, detail on demand.** The first turn of a **Request** sends a **Project** summary: Tracks, **Buses**, the **Master**, their Outputs and **Insert Chain** Effect names, Clips with their positions, **Sections**, the tempo map, and which settings are automated. It leaves out full settings, **Automation** breakpoints and notes. Read tools return those: `read_notes`, `read_channel` (settings and **Insert Chain** in full), `read_automation`. A summary must stay under a stated token budget for a 5-minute, 16-Track song; the budget is measured by a test and written down with the number.
- **Tools in groups.** The always-present core is the reading tools, Tracks, Clips, the mixer basics, `analyse_audio` and `load_tools`. `load_tools(group)` adds a group for the rest of the **Request**: `notes`, `routing`, `automation`, `time`, `sounds`, `audio_clips`, `arrangement`. The context says what each group is for. A group loaded in a **Conversation** stays loaded for its next **Request**.
- **Turns.** The system prompt asks for independent calls (several Tracks' analyses) in one turn. Each result tells the model how many turns it has left. The limit is raised to what the scenarios need, and it is a number in one place. At the limit the model gets one last turn with no tools to write its summary, so the musician is never left without one (#79).
- **Loudness.** The context and `analyse_audio`'s description give a true-peak ceiling of -1 dBTP. Fixing clipping means lowering whatever is actually too loud before the **Master** (#80).

### New tools

- **Notes:** add, delete, move (in time and pitch), resize and set velocity of notes, addressed by the ids `read_notes` returns. Also quantise a Clip or a range to a grid (strength 0–100%), and transpose. Each is one command, like the **Piano Roll's**.
- **Routing:** add, rename and delete a **Bus**, set any channel's Output, add, remove and set the level of a **Send**, and set a **Bus's** volume, pan, mute and solo. A cycle is refused with the same message the UI gives.
- **Automation:** replace a lane's breakpoints over a tick range (with ramps and holds), and clear a lane. Target any automatable setting v2 lists. A setting the **Assistant** sets directly while it is automated still says **Automation** overrides it.
- **Time:** add, move and delete a **Tempo Change**, and set the time signature at a bar line.
- **Sounds:** save the current settings as a User **Preset**, save the Drum Sampler's **Pads** as a **Kit**, and load a saved **Kit** (`set_instrument` stops always loading the Starter Kit).
- **Audio Clips:** place a sample from the library or a **Project** file on an **Audio Track**, trim its start and end, and copy one.
- **Arrangement:** copy Clips (with an offset, onto the same or another Track), insert or delete bars (moving everything after, **Automation** and **Tempo Changes** included), and duplicate or move a **Section**, taking every Track's Clips, **Automation** and **Tempo Changes** in it.

### Sections

- A **Section** is a named, non-overlapping bar range on the timeline, drawn on the ruler, editable by hand (add, rename, drag its edges, delete) and undoable. The ruler also makes the arrangement edits by hand: a **Section** dragged by its body moves with everything in its bars, and its fields duplicate it, move it past a neighbour or delete its bars.
- **Sections** are **Project** data (schema bump), are in the summary, and are what arrangement tools take as their target.

### Conversations

- A **Conversation** is the run of **Requests** since the musician last started a new one. Each **Request** is still one undo step.
- Each follow-up is sent with the earlier **Requests**, the model's replies and a summary of each one's changes, not their full tool traffic, so a **Conversation** doesn't grow without limit. When a **Request** has been undone, the next one is told.
- The **Conversation** is shown as a transcript by the Request box, with a "New conversation" button. Opening or creating a **Project** starts a new one. A **Conversation** is not saved in the **Project**.

### Providers

- The catalogue declares each model's capabilities: tool use, image input, audio input, and several tool calls per turn. Code asks the catalogue, not the provider's name (today `openai.ts` decides images with `who === "OpenAI"`).
- A model without tool use can't be picked for the **Assistant**.
- **Local** models get a smaller core and **Suggestion** mode by default, which the musician can turn off. In **Suggestion** mode the **Request** runs against a copy of the **Project**, and the musician sees the changes listed with an Apply button. Applying is one undo step, and nothing changes until then.

### Listening

- **Native audio.** Where a model declares audio input and the setting allows it, `analyse_audio` can attach the rendered audio (whole mix, one Track, or a range), capped at a length and encoded at a size stated in the tool's description. Other models get the numbers only. It's opt-in per call, like the spectrogram.
- **Before and after.** `analyse_audio` results are kept for the **Request**. `compare_audio` returns the differences between two of them, by default the first and latest of the same target: loudness, peaks, clipping, band energy and onsets.
- **Reference Track.** The musician adds an audio file as the **Project's** **Reference Track**. It is copied into the **Project**, is never in the mix, the meters or an export, and can be auditioned. `compare_to_reference` gives the mix's loudness and band energy against it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] With scripted model replies, every story above runs as one **Request** (or, for 10, two), and one undo reverts each.
- [ ] A summary of the reference 16-Track song is under the recorded token budget, and every tool group's detail is reachable from it.
- [ ] A **Request** that hits the turn limit still ends with a summary.
- [ ] Fixing clipping in the one-loud-Track setup of `real-model-check.ts` lowers that Track, and the result is at or below -1 dBTP (checked with a real model by the maintainer).
- [ ] A follow-up in a **Conversation** acts on the previous **Request's** changes.
- [ ] A **Local** model's **Suggestion** changes nothing until applied, and applying is one undo step.
- [ ] An audio-capable model is sent the audio. Other models aren't.
- [ ] `compare_audio` and `compare_to_reference` report the change a scripted fix made.
- [ ] An MVP or v2 **Project** opens in v3 and renders identically.
- [ ] The real-model check covers the v3 stories and its report is updated (✋).

## Out of scope for v3

- The **Assistant** running a **Request** in the background while the musician edits (the history stays locked during a **Request**).
- Saving **Conversations** in the **Project** or across sessions.
- Generating audio with a model. The **Assistant** arranges the **Project**; it never synthesises sound itself.
- Recording or time-stretching audio by **Request**.
- VST3, installers, collaboration (v4).

## Open questions

- ~~The exact token budget for the summary.~~ Answered by #60: 16,000 tokens for the first message of a **Request** on the reference song in `app/src/assistant/read.test.ts` (5 minutes at 120 BPM, 16 Instrument Tracks of 8-bar Clips, 304 in all, a **Bus**, **Automation** on seven settings and, since #97, 12 **Sections**), asserted there. Tokens are counted as characters over 2.5, on the long side for JSON two fifths UUIDs. It measured about 33,400 characters, or 13,400 tokens, almost all of it the Clips; the **Sections** bring it to about 34,900 characters, or 13,900 tokens, which leaves room for longer names and more **Effects**.
- ~~The turn limit.~~ Answered by #79: `MAX_TURNS` in `app/src/assistant/assistant.ts` is 12 turns with tools, then one more without tools for the summary. The scripted per-Track clipping fix on four Tracks (the mix and each Track, the fix, a check, the Master, a check) takes 9 turns made one call per turn, and 5 with each step's independent calls together, as the system prompt asks. 12 leaves room for a rejected call or an extra check.
- ~~How big a change `compare_audio`'s last line mentions.~~ Answered by #65: `NOTICEABLE_DB` in `app/src/assistant/compare.ts`, 1 dB (or LU) of loudness or band energy, about the smallest change most people hear. Smaller changes are still in its numbers. Clipping and the true peak against the -1 dBTP ceiling are better or worse whatever their size; louder or quieter is neither, since that depends on the **Request**.
- ~~How many files `list_samples` returns.~~ Answered by #105: `MAX_LISTED_SAMPLES` in `app/src/assistant/tools.ts`, 200 files a call across the folders asked for, about 6,000 characters or 2,400 tokens of paths. Past that it says how many more each folder has and asks for a folder or `match` to narrow them, so a sample library of thousands of files never fills the context, and one folder of loops still fits in a call.
- ~~How an arrangement edit joins **Automation** where it cuts the song.~~ Answered by #59: where the value on one side of a cut isn't the value on the other, the lane holds its value up to 1 tick before the cut and steps there (`app/src/project/arrangement.ts`), since a lane has one breakpoint to a tick at whole ticks. A tick is about half a millisecond at 120 BPM, too short to hear, and a lane with no change at the cut gets no breakpoint there, so an edit that moves bars without cutting a ramp leaves it exactly as it was.
- ~~How much of a **Conversation** a follow-up is sent.~~ Answered by #61: `MAX_CONVERSATION_CHARACTERS` in `app/src/assistant/context.ts`, 24,000 characters of earlier **Requests** (what was asked, the **Assistant's** reply and its reported changes), the oldest dropped first and the follow-up told how many were. That is about 9,600 tokens counted as #60 counts them, beside the summary's 16,000. A change is a line of about 40 characters (the drum beat and bassline in `build-a-song.test.ts` is 8 changes in 339), so a long **Conversation** of small **Requests** keeps dozens; even 10 changes in each of 12 turns at 100 characters a line is 12,000, so the **Request** before is always sent whole. A cap in characters rather than **Requests** is what bounds the context, since records differ in size a hundredfold.
- ~~Which tools a **Local** model's smaller core leaves out.~~ Answered by #63: `SMALL_CORE_TOOL_DEFINITIONS` in `app/src/assistant/tools.ts` is the core less `read_automation` (to the automation group, since it is only needed to change **Automation**, and the summary already says what is automated) and `set_track_solo` and `set_master_volume` (to the routing group: `analyse_audio`'s `trackId` hears a Track alone without leaving it soloed, and a small model fixing clipping reaches for the **Master** instead of the loud Track). That is about 1,900 of the core's 12,200 characters of tool definitions, sent every turn, and a test holds it under 85% of the core. Every tool is still one `load_tools` away. The smaller core and **Suggestion** mode are on by default for a provider whose catalogue entry says it runs small models (only **Local**), and **Suggestion** mode can be turned on for any.
- ~~How long the audio `analyse_audio` attaches is, and how it is encoded.~~ Answered by #64: `LISTENING` in `app/src/audio/listening.ts`, the first 30 s of the range asked for, as a 16-bit PCM WAV, mono (the channels averaged) at 16 kHz, made by the engine from the same render the measurements are of. That is about 960 KB, or 1.3 MB base64, an attachment. WAV is the one format every **Provider** that takes audio reads (Gemini's inline audio, OpenAI's `input_audio`, llama.cpp's and Ollama's), and the engine already writes it, so no encoder is added. Gemini hears audio at 16 kbps, mixed to mono, and counts 32 tokens a second, so 16 kHz mono loses nothing it hears and 30 s is 960 tokens; the attachments of a **Request** are re-sent every turn, and 30 s keeps several well under Gemini's 20 MB inline limit. 30 s is 15 bars at 120 BPM, enough to hear a part or a section; a longer range is cut, the result says where, and a later `start` hears the rest, while its measurements stay of the whole range. It is off by default, and saved as `hearAudio` in each **Provider's** settings.
- ~~How loudness matching sets the **Reference Track's** level, and how much of it `compare_to_reference` hears.~~ Answered by #106: matched, it plays at the difference in integrated loudness between the whole mix and the whole reference, as a gain (`matchedGain` in `app/src/reference/reference-player.ts`), and only ever turned down, never above its own level, so a quiet reference is never boosted into clipping. `compare_to_reference` measures the whole reference, whatever range of the mix it is given, decoded at 48 kHz in the UI's WASM build on both platforms, and names the bands whose loudness-matched difference is at least `NOTICEABLE_DB`. A **Local** model's smaller core loads it with the sounds group, so the core stays about a sixth smaller.
- Whether a **Conversation** should survive closing and reopening the app. Left out of v3 and easy to add, since it is not **Project** data.
- ~~How much of the **Project** is sent on each **Request** once songs get large (the MVP's open question).~~ Answered above: a summary, then read tools.
