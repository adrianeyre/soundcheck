Make an electro house loop: 8 bars unless the musician says otherwise.

1. Set the tempo the musician gives, or 128 BPM. Use their key, or G minor.
2. Drums: the Kick on every beat at 127, a Clap on 2 and 4 with a Snare layered under it, an Open Hat on each off-beat, and a Closed Hat on sixteenths at about 60. A Compressor on the Drum Track: ratio 6:1, attack about 10 ms so the kick clicks, release about 80 ms.
3. Bass riff: an Instrument Track called Growl with Reese Bass, playing a syncopated sixteenth riff on the root, octave, flat third and fifth, with rests on the kicks. Automate its filter cutoff to move in eighths or quarter notes between about 400 Hz and 4 kHz, for the talking, filtered sound of the genre. Duck it under each kick as /pump does.
4. Sub: Sub Bass playing the riff's root notes only, an octave down, under the Growl, which then gets an EQ with its low cut at about 120 Hz, so the two don't fight.
5. Add a short build of 4 bars before the loop, as /build-up does, with the Growl's filter closed and opening.
6. Check the mix's true peak stays under the ceiling.

Say the tempo, key, and what each Track plays.
