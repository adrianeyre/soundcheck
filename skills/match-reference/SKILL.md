---
name: match-reference
title: Match the reference
category: Mastering
description: Compare the mix with the Reference Track and move its tone and loudness closer to it.
argument-hint: "[what to focus on, such as low end]"
---

Bring the mix closer to the Reference Track.

1. If the Project has no Reference Track, change nothing and say that one must be added first, from the Transport.
2. Compare the mix to the reference over the whole song, or the Section the musician names.
3. Where a band differs by more than about 2 dB, correct it with a gentle EQ on the Master, or on the one Track that makes that band if it is clearly one. Make no change bigger than 4 dB.
4. If the loudness differs, change the Tracks' volumes rather than pushing the Master, keeping the true peak under the ceiling.
5. Compare to the reference again to check it came closer.

Say what differed, what you changed, and how close it is now.
