---
name: arpeggio
title: Arpeggio
category: Songwriting
description: Turn the song's chords into a rhythmic arpeggio on a pluck, in time with the tempo.
argument-hint: "[rate, such as 1/16] [pattern: up, down, up-down]"
---

Play the song's chords as an arpeggio.

1. Read the notes of the chords Track (or the Pattern Clips with the most notes sounding at once) to find the chord in each bar. With none, use A minor and i–VI–III–VII.
2. Add an Instrument Track called Arp with the Synth on Synth Pluck, or Bell Pluck for something brighter, or use the Arp Track the Project has.
3. For each bar, take its chord's notes, add the root an octave up, and play them one at a time at the rate the musician names: sixteenths unless they say otherwise. Go up, down or up and down as they say; up and down without repeating the top and bottom notes, if they say nothing.
4. Keep each note a little shorter than the step, so they don't blur. Accent the first note of every beat with velocity around 105 and play the rest around 80.
5. Put it an octave above the chords, so the two don't fight.
6. If the Project has a Delay on a Bus, send the Arp to it at about 0.2; otherwise add a Delay to the Arp itself from its "Dotted-eighth ping-pong" preset with its mix low.

Say the rate, the pattern and how many bars you wrote.
