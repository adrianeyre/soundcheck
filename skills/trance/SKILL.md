Make a trance loop: 16 bars unless the musician says otherwise. For psytrance, follow /psytrance instead.

1. Set the tempo the musician gives, or 138 BPM (132 for progressive). Use their key, or A minor with the progression i–VI–III–VII, one chord to 2 bars, which is trance's classic lift; for progressive, stay on one or two chords.
2. Drums: the Kick on every beat, a Clap on 2 and 4, an Open Hat on every off-beat eighth, and a Closed Hat on sixteenths at about 60 between them. A snare roll into every 8th bar, as /build-up does.
3. Bass: an Instrument Track called Bass with Sub Bass, playing the chord's root on the three sixteenths after each kick (the rolling "gallop" of trance), or on the off-beat eighths for progressive. Duck it under the kick as /pump does.
4. Pad: Warm Pad holding each chord, voiced in the octave above middle C.
5. Lead: an Instrument Track called Supersaw with Saw Lead, playing an emotive melody over the chords as /melody does, with long notes and a peak that lifts in bar 7 of each 8. Add a Delay synced to a dotted eighth (the "Dotted-eighth ping-pong" preset) on it, and send it to a Reverb Bus with a large hall (size 0.9, decay about 4 s, pre-delay 40 ms).
6. Arp: add a sixteenth-note arpeggio of the chords, as /arpeggio does, on Synth Pluck, with its filter cutoff automated to open over each 8 bars.
7. If the song has room, add a Section called Breakdown of 16 bars after the loop, with only the pad, lead and arp, then a Build of 8 bars into the loop again, as /breakdown and /build-up do.

Say the tempo, key, chords, and what each Track plays.
