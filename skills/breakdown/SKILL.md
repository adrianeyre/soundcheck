---
name: breakdown
title: Breakdown
category: Arrangement
description: Strip a Section back to its chords and melody, then bring the drums back in for the next Section.
argument-hint: "[Section]"
---

Turn a Section into a breakdown: the one the musician names, or the Section before the last chorus or drop; with no Sections, bars 17 to 24.

1. Find the Section's ticks, and read the Project for the Clips on each Track in it.
2. Take the kick and bass out of the Section: delete their Clips that lie wholly inside it, and for any that cross its edges, automate that Track's volume to 0 from the Section's start, holding, and back to its volume now at the next Section's start.
3. Keep the chords and the lead, and give them room: on the chords Track, automate the Synth's filter cutoff down to about 800 Hz at the Section's start, opening back up over its last 4 bars. Raise its Send to a Reverb Bus, if there is one, over the same bars.
4. Keep the hi-hats or percussion only in the last 4 bars, if there are any, so the drums return gradually.
5. Leave the Section after it as it is, so everything returns on its first beat.

Say what plays in the breakdown and what comes back after it.
