---
name: drum-beat
title: Drum beat
category: Drums
description: Program a drum beat in a genre and tempo you name, on a new or existing Drum Track.
argument-hint: "[genre] [tempo] [bars]"
---

Program a drum beat as the musician describes it: a genre or feel, and optionally a tempo and a length in bars. Without a genre, make a straight four-on-the-floor beat; without a length, make it 4 bars.

1. Use the Drum Track the Project already has, or add one with a Drum Sampler if it has none. Read its channel for the pitch each Pad plays.
2. If the musician gave a tempo, set it.
3. Add one Pattern Clip at the top of the song, or after the last Clip on that Track, and write its notes with set_pattern_notes: kick, snare or clap, and closed and open hi-hats, as the genre plays them.
4. Give the hi-hats some movement: vary their velocities so the off-beats are softer than the on-beats, and put an open hi-hat before the bar ends.
5. Leave the rest of the song alone.

Say what you programmed in one sentence, such as the genre, tempo and length.
