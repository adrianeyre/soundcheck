---
name: pump
title: Pumping duck
category: Mixing
description: Duck pads, chords and bass on every kick, so the mix pumps with the beat and the kick cuts through.
argument-hint: "[Tracks] [depth: light or heavy] [Section]"
---

Make Tracks duck under the kick with volume Automation, as a sidechained compressor would.

1. Read the Drum Track's notes for where the kick plays. Without a Drum Track, use every beat.
2. Pick the Tracks: those the musician names, or the pads, chords and bass. Read each one's channel for its volume.
3. Over the Section the musician names, or the whole song where the kick plays, draw each Track's volume Automation with set_automation: at each kick, drop to about half its volume (a quarter for heavy, three quarters for light), then ramp back to its full volume by the next off-beat eighth. Hold the full volume until the next kick.
4. Duck the bass for a shorter time, back up after a sixteenth, so its note still comes through.
5. Where a Track is already automated, say so and leave it.

Say which Tracks now pump, how deep, and over which bars.
