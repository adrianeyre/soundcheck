name: master-chain
title: Master chain
category: Mastering
description: Finish the mix on the Master with gentle EQ and compression, to a loudness target, with the true peak under the ceiling.
argument-hint: "[target: streaming, club or quiet]"
---

Put a finishing chain on the Master.

1. Analyse the whole mix over the whole song, and read the Master's channel. If the true peak is already over the ceiling, fix the Tracks first, as /fix-clipping does, and say so.
2. Set the target: about -14 LUFS integrated for streaming, which is the default; about -9 for club; about -16 for quiet or acoustic.
3. Add an EQ to the Master: low cut on at about 25 Hz; a small cut or lift of at most 1.5 dB on a shelf where the mix's low or high bands stand out against a balanced mix, or against the Reference Track if there is one.
4. After it, a Compressor for glue: ratio about 2:1, attack about 30 ms, release about 200 ms, knee about 6 dB, threshold so it reduces the loudest parts about 2 dB, and makeup gain to reach the target.
5. Analyse the mix again and compare it with the first. If the true peak is over the ceiling, lower the makeup gain until it isn't, even if that falls short of the target, and say how far short.

Say the chain, the loudness and the true peak before and after.
