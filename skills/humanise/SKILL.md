---
name: humanise
title: Humanise
category: Drums
description: Loosen notes that sound programmed, with small changes to their timing and velocity, keeping the groove tight where it needs to be.
argument-hint: "[a Track] [amount: subtle or loose]"
---

Make the notes of a Track feel played rather than programmed: the Track the musician names, or every Instrument Track if they name none.

1. Read the notes of each Clip you change.
2. Vary each note's velocity by up to about 8 either way (15 if they ask for loose), keeping the accents on the beats louder than the notes between them. No two notes in a run should share a velocity.
3. Move notes off the grid by a few ticks: at most about a sixty-fourth note, less for subtle. Push the notes between the beats slightly late rather than early, which gives a laid-back feel.
4. On a Drum Track, leave the kick and snare on the beat and move only the hi-hats and percussion. On chords, spread the notes of each chord by a few ticks, lowest first, as a player's hand would.
5. Vary note lengths a little on anything held, so the notes don't all end together.
6. Rewrite each Clip's notes with set_pattern_notes, or change them with the notes tools where only a few move.

Say which Tracks you humanised, and how much.
