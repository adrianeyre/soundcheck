Make a happy hardcore loop: 16 bars unless the musician says otherwise.

1. Set the tempo the musician gives, or 170 BPM (165 to 180). Use their key, or C major, with I–V–vi–IV, one chord to a bar.
2. Drums: the Kick on every beat at 127; a Clap on 2 and 4; an Open Hat on each off-beat; a snare roll into every 8th bar, as /build-up does.
3. Bass: Sub Bass or Reese Bass playing the root on each off-beat eighth, bouncing between the kicks; duck it under each kick as /pump does.
4. Piano: an Instrument Track called Piano with Electric Keys, playing the chords as stabs in a syncopated rave rhythm, such as on the "and" of 1, 2 and the "e" of 4, in the octave above middle C.
5. Lead: Square Lead playing a bright, catchy major-key melody that rises to its peak in bar 7 of each 8, as /melody does, doubled an octave up at a lower velocity, with a Delay synced to a dotted eighth.
6. Send the piano and lead to a Reverb Bus with a big hall.

Say the tempo, key, chords, and what each Track plays.
