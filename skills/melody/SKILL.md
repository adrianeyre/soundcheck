---
name: melody
title: Lead melody
category: Songwriting
description: Write a singable lead melody over the song's chords, with a hook that repeats and room to breathe.
argument-hint: "[Section] [style, such as catchy or sparse]"
---

Write a lead melody over the chords, in the Section the musician names or over the first 8 bars that have chords.

1. Read the notes of the chords, bass and any existing lead to find the key and the chord in each bar. With no chords, use A minor.
2. Add an Instrument Track called Lead with the Synth on Saw Lead, Square Lead or Bell Pluck, as the style suits, or use the lead Track the Project has.
3. Write a hook of 2 bars, then answer it: play the hook, change its ending, play it again, then end the phrase on the key's root or third. This call and response is what makes a melody stick.
4. Land on a note of the bar's chord on beats 1 and 3; move between them mostly by step, with at most one leap in a phrase, followed by a step back the other way.
5. Keep it within about an octave and a half, above the chords, around the C above middle C. Leave rests: at least a beat of space at the end of each 2-bar phrase, and not a note on every sixteenth.
6. Make the highest note of the melody fall once, near the end of the phrase, as its peak. Accent the beats with velocity around 100 and the notes between them nearer 80.

Say the key, the range, and how the hook goes, in one or two sentences.
