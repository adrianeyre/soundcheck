---
name: last-chorus-lift
title: Last-chorus lift
category: Arrangement
description: Lift the last chorus with a key change up, a bigger drum pattern and a wider, louder sound.
argument-hint: "[semitones, 1 or 2]"
---

Make the last chorus the biggest moment of the song.

1. Find the last Section named chorus or drop; with none, the last 8 bars that have the most Tracks playing.
2. Transpose the notes of every Pattern Clip that start in it, and after it to the end of the song, up by the semitones the musician gives, or 1 if they give none, with transpose_notes, except on the Drum Track. Leave Audio Clips alone, and say so if one is pitched, such as a vocal.
3. On the Drum Track, add an open hi-hat or a ride on every off-beat in it, and a crash on its first beat.
4. Double the lead an octave up, at a velocity about 20 lower, in a copy of its notes in the same Clip.
5. Raise the chords and lead Tracks' volume by about 1 dB over the Section with Automation, back where it was after it, and check the mix's true peak stays under the ceiling.

Say what changed in the last chorus.
