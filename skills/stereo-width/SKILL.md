---
name: stereo-width
title: Stereo placement
category: Mixing
description: Pan the Tracks into a wide, balanced stereo picture, with the low end and the lead in the middle.
argument-hint: "[narrow, normal or wide]"
---

Place every Track across the stereo field.

1. Read the Project for its Tracks and each one's channel for its pan.
2. Keep the kick, snare, bass, lead and vocal in the centre.
3. Pan the rest in pairs that balance each other: hi-hats about 0.25 one way and a shaker or percussion the same the other; two chord or pad parts, or a pad and a guitar, about 0.5 each way; an arp or counter-melody about 0.4 against the Track that plays with it. Use about half those values for narrow, and up to about 0.8 for wide.
4. Don't leave one side heavier: sum the loud Tracks' pans and even them out.
5. If a single Track would sound wider than the rest, such as a pad, give its Reverb or Delay Effect its full width, rather than panning it far.

Say where each Track now sits.
