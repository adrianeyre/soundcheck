Make a UK garage loop: 8 bars unless the musician says otherwise.

1. Set the tempo the musician gives, or 132 BPM (130 to 135). Use their key, or E minor, with minor seventh chords.
2. Drums, 2-step: the Kick on beat 1 and the "a" of 2 (a sixteenth before 3), moving the second kick in alternate bars; the Snare and Clap on 2 and 4; a Closed Hat on sixteenths, heavily swung, moving every second sixteenth about half a sixteenth late; a Cowbell or rim sound on a skippy syncopated line. For speed garage, use a kick on every beat instead.
3. Swing everything the same amount, and humanise the velocities as /humanise does.
4. Bass: Sub Bass, playing short, syncopated notes that bounce between the root and its octave, locked to the kick; for speed garage, Reese Bass with a wobbling filter.
5. Chords: Electric Keys playing chopped chords on off-beat sixteenths, short, swung, with a Delay synced to an eighth.
6. Add a short vocal-like hook on Bell Pluck, two or three notes, to stand in for the chopped vocal the genre uses.

Say the tempo, key, chords, and what each Track plays.
