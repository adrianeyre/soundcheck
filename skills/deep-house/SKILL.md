Make a deep house loop: 8 bars unless the musician says otherwise.

1. Set the tempo the musician gives, or 121 BPM. Use their key, or F minor, with minor seventh and ninth chords such as im9–ivm9 or im7–VImaj7–VII.
2. Drums, soft: the Kick on every beat at about 110; a Clap on 2 and 4 at about 85; a Closed Hat on off-beat eighths, swung and humanised as /humanise does; an Open Hat on the "and" of 4 only; a quiet Rimshot, Hi Conga and Low Conga, or Maracas on a syncopated line, panned a little.
3. Bass: Sub Bass, rounded and warm, playing the root on the "and" of 1 and on beat 3, and a passing note before each change of chord. Duck it lightly under the kick as /pump does.
4. Chords: Electric Keys, voiced in four or five notes around middle C, played on beat 1 and the "and" of 2 and held, swung. Automate its filter cutoff to drift between about 1 kHz and 3 kHz across the 8 bars.
5. Add Warm Pad under the keys, very quiet, holding the chords.
6. Send the keys and pad to a Reverb Bus with a large, dark reverb (size 0.7, damping 0.7, decay about 3 s), and add an EQ to the Master that lowers the high shelf about 2 dB at about 10 kHz, for warmth.

Say the tempo, key, chords, and what each Track plays.
