Make a techno loop: 16 bars unless the musician says otherwise. Techno is repetition that changes slowly.

1. Set the tempo the musician gives, or 130 BPM (125 for minimal, 135 to 140 for peak-time, 140 to 150 for industrial). Use their key, or A minor, played as one note or chord, not a progression.
2. Drums: the Kick on every beat; a Closed Hat on every sixteenth, off-beats louder; an Open Hat on each off-beat eighth; a Clap on 2 and 4, or only on 4 for minimal; the Cowbell or a tom on a 3-sixteenth pattern that crosses the bar line, for polyrhythm.
3. Rumble: an Instrument Track called Rumble with Sub Bass playing the root on the three sixteenths after each kick, very low, and send it to a Reverb Bus with a dark, long reverb (damping 0.9, decay about 4 s) with an EQ on the Bus cutting everything above about 200 Hz, then duck it under the kick as /pump does.
4. Hook: one bar, on Synth Pluck or Reese Bass, a few sixteenth notes on the root and its octave, repeating every bar. Automate its filter cutoff to open slowly over the 16 bars, and a Delay synced to a dotted eighth.
5. Add something every 4 bars, such as a hat pattern or the clap, and take it away 4 bars later, so the loop keeps moving.
6. For industrial, add an EQ on the Drum Track boosting about 3 dB at about 3 kHz, and use a distortion Plugin if one is installed.

Say the tempo, key, and how the loop changes over its bars.
