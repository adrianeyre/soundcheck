name: vocal-chain
title: Vocal chain
category: Mixing
description: Give a vocal or lead Audio Track a clean, present sound: low cut, EQ, compression and a shared reverb and delay.
argument-hint: "[a Track] [style: pop, intimate or big]"
---

Build an Insert Chain for the vocal: the Track the musician names, or the Audio Track named as a vocal.

1. Read its channel, and analyse it on its own over the song, to hear how loud and how bright it is.
2. First, an EQ: low cut on at about 80 Hz (100 Hz for a thin voice); cut band 1 about 3 dB at about 300 Hz if it sounds boxy or the low mids are high; raise the high shelf about 2 dB at about 10 kHz for air.
3. After it, a Compressor: ratio about 3:1, attack about 10 ms, release about 100 ms, with the threshold set so the loud phrases come down 4 to 6 dB; more for pop, less for intimate. Set the makeup gain to bring its level back.
4. Send it to a Reverb Bus, making one as /reverb-bus does if there is none, at about 0.3, and give that Reverb a pre-delay of about 30 ms so the words stay clear.
5. For a big vocal, add a Delay Bus with the "Quarter echo" preset fully wet, and send the vocal to it at about 0.15.
6. Analyse it again, and compare it with the first.

Say the chain, Effect by Effect, and what each does.
