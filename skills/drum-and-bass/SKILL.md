Make a drum and bass loop: 16 bars unless the musician says otherwise.

1. Set the tempo the musician gives, or 174 BPM (170 to 176). Use their key, or F minor.
2. Drums, two-step: the Kick on beat 1 and the "and" of 3; the Snare on 2 and 4 at 120; a Closed Hat on eighths with ghost snares at about 45 on a few sixteenths between; an Open Hat on the "and" of 4 now and then. For jungle, chop the pattern with extra snares and toms on sixteenths every other bar.
3. Bass: an Instrument Track called Reese with Reese Bass, playing long notes on the root and the flat sixth or flat seventh, changing every 2 bars, with its filter cutoff automated to move slowly. For liquid, use Sub Bass with rounder notes under the chords. Add an EQ with its low cut at about 30 Hz, and a Compressor, ratio about 4:1.
4. Pad: Warm Pad (liquid) or Glass Pad (neuro), holding minor seventh chords, one to 4 bars, sent to a Reverb Bus with a long hall (decay about 4 s).
5. For liquid, add Electric Keys playing the chords softly, humanised.
6. Duck the Reese under each kick and snare a little, as /pump does.

Say the tempo, key, and what each Track plays.
