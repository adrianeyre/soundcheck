Make a psytrance loop: 16 bars unless the musician says otherwise.

1. Set the tempo the musician gives, or 145 BPM (138 for progressive psy, 148 to 150 for dark). Use their key, or E minor or E Phrygian, played on one note.
2. Drums: the Kick on every beat, short and tight; an Open Hat on each off-beat; a Closed Hat on sixteenths at about 60; a Clap on 2 and 4, or none for progressive.
3. Bass, the heart of the genre: an Instrument Track called Rolling Bass with Sub Bass or Reese Bass, playing the root on the three sixteenths after each kick, never on the kick itself, and staccato: each note a little shorter than a sixteenth. Move one note of every 4 bars to the flat second or octave. Its volume must never overlap the kick: duck it as /pump does if the notes are longer.
4. Acid: an Instrument Track called Acid with Saw Lead or Square Lead, playing a sixteenth-note line on the root, flat second, minor third and octave, with its filter cutoff and resonance automated to sweep slowly over each 8 bars. For dark psy, keep the cutoff low and the line faster.
5. Add a sweeping Glass Pad or effect at the start of every 8 bars, with a long reverb and a ping-pong Delay.
6. Change something every 8 bars, such as a new acid line or the filter's range, to keep it hypnotic without looping.

Say the tempo, key, and what each Track plays.
