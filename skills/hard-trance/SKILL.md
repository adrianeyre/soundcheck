Make a hard trance loop: 16 bars unless the musician says otherwise. It is trance's chords and energy with a harder, faster, more relentless drive; for uplifting trance follow /trance, and for hardstyle's reverse bass follow /hardstyle.

1. Set the tempo the musician gives, or 145 BPM (modern runs 148 to 155). Use their key, or A minor with i–VI–VII–i, one chord to 2 bars; for classic, stay darker on i–VII over 4 bars.
2. Drums: the Starter Kit's Hard Kick on every beat at 127, with a Clipper after it (input about 6 dB, ceiling -1 dB, a little softness) so it hits loud without clipping the Master. A Clap on 2 and 4, an Open Hat on every off-beat eighth, Closed Hats on sixteenths at about 60 between them, and a Ride on the off-beats in the second 8 bars. A Crash on the first beat of every 8 bars, and a snare roll into every 16th bar, as /build-up does.
3. Bass: an Instrument Track called Bass with Sub Bass, playing the chord's root on the three sixteenths after each kick (trance's rolling gallop). Put a Pump on it synced to a quarter note at a depth of about 0.8, so it ducks under every kick; keep it mono below about 120 Hz, as /clean-low-end does.
4. Stab: an Instrument Track called Stab with Saw Lead, holding each chord in the octave above middle C, through a Trance Gate on sixteenths with a driving pattern, then a Reverb Bus send (size 0.6, decay about 2 s). This is the stuttering chord that carries hard trance.
5. Lead: an Instrument Track called Lead with Saw Lead, playing a short, hooky, repeating riff of sixteenths and eighths over the chords, as /melody does, with its filter cutoff automated to open across each 8 bars and a Saturator for bite. Add a Delay synced to a dotted eighth. For modern, double it an octave up at a lower velocity.
6. Acid line (classic): an Instrument Track called Acid with Reese Bass an octave up, on sixteenths with slides between some notes, its filter cutoff and resonance automated, through a Saturator.
7. If the song has room, add a Section called Breakdown of 16 bars after the loop, with only the stab (gate off) and lead over the pad-like held chords, then a Build of 8 bars into the loop again, with a Beat Repeat on the drums in its last bar, as /breakdown and /build-up do.

Say the tempo, key, chords, and what each Track plays.
