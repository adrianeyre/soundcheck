Make a house loop: 8 bars unless the musician says otherwise. For deeper, moodier house, follow /deep-house.

1. Set the tempo the musician gives, or 124 BPM (125 to 127 for tech house). Use their key, or C minor, with i–iv or i–VII–VI.
2. Drums: the Kick on every beat; a Clap on 2 and 4; an Open Hat on every off-beat eighth; a Closed Hat on sixteenths, swung: move every second sixteenth about a third of a sixteenth late. Add the Cowbell, a Rimshot or a tom on a syncopated line for tech house, and a Tambourine on the off-beats or Congas for a latin or classic feel.
3. Bass: an Instrument Track called Bass with Sub Bass, playing the root on off-beats with an octave jump on the last sixteenth of every other bar, swung as the hats are.
4. Chords: Electric Keys, as /chords does, played as short stabs on syncopated sixteenths, such as the "and" of 1 and the "e" of 3, swung. For soulful house, add Warm Pad holding the chords.
5. Humanise the hats and keys a little, as /humanise does.
6. Send the keys to a Reverb Bus with a room (size 0.4, decay about 1.2 s).

Say the tempo, key, chords, and what each Track plays.
