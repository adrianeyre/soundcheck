Make a dubstep loop: 8 bars unless the musician says otherwise.

1. Set the tempo the musician gives, or 140 BPM, played in half time. Use their key, or F minor.
2. Drums: the Kick on beat 1, and sometimes on the "a" of 2; the Snare and Clap together on beat 3 at 127 every bar; a Closed Hat on eighths or triplets, with a few rolls in the last bar of every 4. Lots of space: dubstep is about the gaps.
3. Sub: Sub Bass playing the root under the kick and wobble, long notes, mono and centred.
4. Wobble: an Instrument Track called Wobble with Reese Bass, playing the same notes as the sub an octave up. Automate its filter cutoff to swing between about 200 Hz and 3 kHz at the rate the musician gives: an eighth unless they say otherwise, with bars of triplets or sixteenths for variation. Give it an EQ with its low cut at about 100 Hz, so the sub owns the low end.
5. Leave the wobble silent on beat 3 in some bars, so the snare hits alone.
6. Add a Glass Pad with a long reverb in the background, very quiet, for atmosphere.

Say the tempo, key, the wobble's rate, and what each Track plays.
