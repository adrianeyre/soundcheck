Make a dance loop, as festival EDM and pop dance play it: an 8-bar build and a 16-bar drop unless the musician says otherwise.

1. Set the tempo the musician gives, or 128 BPM (126 for pop dance, 124 for future house). Use their key, or F minor with vi–IV–I–V (as Dm–Bb–F–C in F major), one chord to a bar.
2. Drums for the drop: the Kick on every beat, a Clap on 2 and 4 with a Snare layered under it at about 70, an Open Hat on each off-beat, and a Closed Hat on sixteenths at about 55. For future house, add a Cowbell or percussion on a syncopated sixteenth rhythm.
3. Bass: Sub Bass on the root of each chord, on the off-beats, ducked under each kick as /pump does, deeply. For future house, use Reese Bass and a bouncier rhythm with octave jumps.
4. Chords: Warm Pad or Electric Keys, as /chords does, pumping with the kick as /pump does.
5. Hook: an Instrument Track called Hook with Synth Pluck playing a short, catchy 2-bar riff on the chord tones, repeated with a change at the end of every 4 bars. Send it to a Reverb Bus and a Delay synced to a dotted eighth.
6. Mark two Sections: Build (8 bars) and Drop (16). In the Build, drop the kick, keep the chords and a filtered hook, and make a build-up as /build-up does, ending with a beat of silence. In the Drop, everything plays.
7. Check the mix's true peak stays under the ceiling.

Say the tempo, key, chords, and what plays in the Build and the Drop.
