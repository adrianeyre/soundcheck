---
name: clean-low-end
title: Clean the low end
category: Mixing
description: Clear muddiness by cutting lows from the Tracks that don't need them, so the kick and bass come through.
argument-hint: "[a Track or Section to focus on]"
---

Clean up the mix's low end and low mids.

1. Analyse the whole mix and each Track on its own, over the Section the musician names or the whole song, in one turn, and look at the energy per band.
2. On every Track but the kick and bass, add an EQ (or change the one it has) with its low cut on: about 150 Hz for chords, pads and leads, 250 Hz for hi-hats, percussion and anything bright, 80 Hz for a vocal.
3. If the mix is still heavy around 200 to 400 Hz, find the Track with most energy there besides the bass, and cut its band 1 at about 300 Hz by 2 to 4 dB with a Q of about 1.5.
4. If the kick and bass fight, give each a space: cut the bass by about 3 dB where the kick's low band peaks (around 60 to 80 Hz), or the kick where the bass is.
5. Analyse the mix again over the same range and compare it with the first, in the same turn.

Say which Tracks you cut, where and by how much, and how the low bands changed.
