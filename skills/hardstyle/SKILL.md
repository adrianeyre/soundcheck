Make a hardstyle loop: 16 bars unless the musician says otherwise.

1. Set the tempo the musician gives, or 150 BPM (raw runs to 155 to 160). Use their key, or A minor with i–VI–III–VII, one chord to 2 bars.
2. Drums: the Starter Kit's Hard Kick on every beat at 127 (the Kick, where the Drum Sampler has no Hard Kick), with an EQ boosting about 4 dB at about 60 Hz and a Compressor at a fast attack. The Hard Kick is already distorted, with a long tail; with only the Kick, use a distortion Plugin or a hardstyle Kit if one is available, and otherwise say the built-in Effects can't distort it as far. Add a Clap on 2 and 4, and an Open Hat on each off-beat.
3. Reverse bass: an Instrument Track called Reverse Bass with Sub Bass, playing the chord's root on each off-beat eighth, between the kicks, with its volume automated to rise across each off-beat, from low at its start to full at its end, so it swells into the next kick.
4. Lead: an Instrument Track called Lead with Saw Lead, playing a big, singable melody with long held notes over the chords, as /melody does. Double it an octave up at a lower velocity. Send it to a Reverb Bus with a big hall (size 0.9, decay about 4 s) and a Delay synced to a dotted eighth.
5. For raw, replace the melody with a short, dark screech riff on Reese Bass an octave up, on sixteenths, with its filter automated.
6. Add Warm Pad holding the chords under the lead.

Say the tempo, key, chords, and what each Track plays.
