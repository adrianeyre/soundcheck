---
name: drum-fill
title: Drum fills
category: Drums
description: Add drum fills at the ends of phrases and into each Section, so the beat doesn't loop.
argument-hint: "[every 4 or 8 bars] [style, such as tom roll or snare]"
---

Add drum fills to the Drum Track.

1. Read the Drum Track's channel for the pitch each Pad plays, and the notes of its Clips. If there is no Drum Track, change nothing and say a beat must be written first, as /drum-beat does.
2. Find where fills go: the last bar before each Section starts, and, where the musician says, the last bar of every 4 or 8. Without Sections and without a number, every 8 bars.
3. In each of those bars, keep the first two beats of the beat as they are, and replace the last one or two beats with the fill: sixteenths on the snare, or toms from high to low if the Kit has them, rising in velocity from about 70 to 115. Make the fill before a Section a full two beats, and the ones inside a Section a single beat.
4. Drop the hi-hats under the fill, and end every fill with a kick and a crash (or an open hi-hat, if there is no crash Pad) on the first beat of the next bar.
5. Where the fill falls inside a Clip that repeats elsewhere, change only that bar's notes, keeping the rest.

Say how many fills you added, and where.
