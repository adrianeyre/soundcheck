---
name: fades
title: Fade in and out
category: Arrangement
description: Fade the song in at its start and out at its end, or fade one Track in or out over a Section.
argument-hint: "[in, out or both] [bars] [a Track]"
---

Draw fades with volume Automation.

1. Without a Track named, fade the whole song on the Master; with one, fade that Track. Read its channel for its volume now.
2. For a fade-in, ramp its volume from 0 at the start up to its volume now over the bars the musician gives: 4 bars unless they say otherwise.
3. For a fade-out, ramp from its volume now down to 0 over the bars before the end of the last Clip: 8 bars unless they say otherwise. Hold the value from the fade's start so nothing changes before it.
4. Draw long fades in several breakpoints that fall faster at first and slow towards 0 for a fade-out (and the reverse for a fade-in), so the fade sounds even rather than dropping away at the end.
5. If the setting is already automated, change its Automation over the fade's bars only.

Say what fades, from where to where.
