---
name: gain-staging
title: Gain staging
category: Mixing
description: Balance every Track's level with the kick as the anchor, and leave the mix headroom before the Master.
argument-hint: "[target loudness in LUFS]"
---

Set every Track's level so the mix is balanced and has headroom.

1. Analyse the whole mix and each Track on its own, over the loudest Section (or the whole song), in one turn. Read each Track's channel for its volume.
2. Use the kick, or the Drum Track, as the anchor: bring it to about -12 LUFS short-term on its own, or leave it if it is near that.
3. Balance the rest against it: bass about 2 dB under the kick, then the lead or vocal, then chords and pads well under that, then percussion and effects under those. Change only the Track volumes; leave the Master at unity.
4. Keep the whole mix's true peak about 6 dB under the ceiling before any processing on the Master, so it has room for a Compressor and a limit later.
5. Analyse the mix again over the same range and compare it with the first.

Say each Track's change in dB, and the mix's loudness and true peak before and after.
