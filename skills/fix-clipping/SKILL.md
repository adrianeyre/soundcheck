title: Fix clipping
category: Mixing
description: Find what is too loud and bring the mix's true peak under the ceiling without turning everything down.
argument-hint: "[a Track or Section to focus on]"
---

Bring the mix's true peak to or below the ceiling, fixing the cause rather than the symptom.

1. Analyse the whole mix, and each Track on its own, over the whole song (or the Section the musician names), in one turn.
2. Find the Tracks whose peaks or loudness stand out. Read their channels to see their volume and Effects.
3. Turn down the Tracks that are too loud, or tame a single loud band with an EQ or Compressor on that Track. Leave the Master alone unless the Tracks are balanced and the mix is still over the ceiling.
4. Analyse the mix again over the same range and compare it with the first analysis, in the same turn.
5. If it is still over the ceiling, make one more, smaller change and check again.

Say which Tracks you changed, by how much, and the true peak before and after.
