---
name: gabber
title: Gabber
description: Start a Rotterdam-style gabber loop at about 190 BPM: a distorted kick on every beat whose tail plays the riff, and a hoover lead.
category: Genres
argument-hint: "[tempo] [key] [bars]"
---

Make a gabber loop: 8 bars unless the musician says otherwise, at the top of the song or after the last Clip.

1. Set the tempo the musician gives, or 190 BPM (gabber runs from about 170 to 220). Use their key, or E minor.
2. Drums, on a Drum Track: the Kick on every beat at velocity 127. Gabber is the kick above everything: add only a Closed Hat on the off-beats at about 70, and a Clap on beats 2 and 4 in the second half of the loop. No swing.
3. The kick is distorted until it sounds like a tone. If one of the musician's saved Kits has a gabber kick, load it; if a distortion or bitcrusher Plugin is installed, add it to the Drum Track. After it add an EQ with its low cut at about 30 Hz, low shelf about +5 dB at about 55 Hz and band 3 about +4 dB at about 3 kHz, then a Compressor at ratio 20:1, attack 0.5 ms, release 40 ms, with high makeup gain. Without a distorting Plugin or Kit, say the built-in Effects can't distort it and a gabber kick sample or Plugin is needed.
4. The kick's tail plays the riff: add an Instrument Track called Kick Tone with the Synth on Sub Bass, and on every kick play a note of a riff that moves between the root, the flat second, the minor third and the fifth, an eighth long, so the pitch of each kick follows it. Change the riff every 2 bars. Keep it well under the Drum Track, as the tone the kick sings, not a separate bass.
5. Lead: an Instrument Track called Hoover with the Synth on Reese Bass an octave up, or Saw Lead, playing a menacing line of long notes that slide between the root and flat second, with its filter cutoff automated to open over each 4 bars. Send it to a Reverb Bus with a big hall (size 0.8, decay about 3 s).
6. Keep the mix's true peak under the ceiling: the kick is loud, so turn down the rest to it.

Say the tempo, key, and what each Track plays.
