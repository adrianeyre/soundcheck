Make an electro loop in the Detroit and electro-funk style: 8 bars unless the musician says otherwise. If they ask for electro house, follow /electro-house instead.

1. Set the tempo the musician gives, or 125 BPM (electro runs from about 110 to 135). Use their key, or E minor.
2. Drums: electro is not four-on-the-floor. Put the Kick on beat 1, the "and" of 2 and the "a" of 3 (a sixteenth before 4), varying the last kick of every other bar; the Snare or Clap on 2 and 4; a Closed Hat on every sixteenth, accenting the off-beats; the Cowbell on a syncopated line; Low Tom and High Tom answering each other in the last bar of every 4.
3. Quantise it hard, no swing: electro is machine funk.
4. Bass: an Instrument Track called Bass with Sub Bass (or Square Lead an octave down), playing a funky sixteenth-note line of short notes on the root, octave and flat seventh, locking with the kick and leaving the snare's beats clear.
5. Lead: an Instrument Track called Robot with Square Lead, playing a short, repeating riff of sixteenths in the minor pentatonic scale, with its filter cutoff automated low and opening in the second 4 bars.
6. Pad: Glass Pad holding one or two minor chords, quiet, for a cold, futuristic feel, with a Delay on the "Quarter echo" preset.
7. Keep each part dry except the pad; electro is tight and upfront.

Say the tempo, key, and what each Track plays.
