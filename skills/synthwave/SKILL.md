Make a synthwave loop: 16 bars unless the musician says otherwise.

1. Set the tempo the musician gives, or 100 BPM (outrun runs faster, up to 118; dreamwave slower, about 85). Use their key, or A minor with i–VI–III–VII, one chord to 2 bars.
2. Drums: the Kick on 1 and 3; the Snare on 2 and 4 at 120; a Closed Hat on eighths or sixteenths; a Low Tom and High Tom fill every 8 bars. Give the snare its 80s sound: send it to a Reverb Bus with a big, bright reverb (size 0.8, decay about 2 s, damping 0.2), and automate the Send so each hit's reverb cuts off after an eighth, for the gated reverb of the decade.
3. Bass: an Instrument Track called Bass with Sub Bass or Square Lead an octave down, playing the chord's root on every eighth note, the driving pulse of the genre, alternating with its octave in the last beat of each bar.
4. Pad: Warm Pad holding each chord, wide, with a long reverb.
5. Lead: Square Lead or Saw Lead playing a nostalgic, simple melody over the chords, as /melody does, with the "Quarter echo" Delay preset and plenty of reverb. For dreamwave, use Glass Pad and Bell Pluck instead.
6. Add Synth Pluck playing an eighth-note arpeggio of the chords, as /arpeggio does.

Say the tempo, key, chords, and what each Track plays.
