Make a trap beat: 8 bars unless the musician says otherwise.

1. Set the tempo the musician gives, or 140 BPM, felt in half time (a bar sounds like two). Use their key, or C minor, often with the flat second or the harmonic minor's raised seventh for a dark sound.
2. Drums: the Kick sparse and syncopated, such as on 1 and the "a" of 2; the Snare and Clap on beat 3 of each bar; a Closed Hat on eighths, with rolls: runs of sixteenths, sixteenth triplets and thirty-seconds in places, rising and falling in velocity; an Open Hat once every 2 bars.
3. 808: an Instrument Track called 808 with Sub Bass, playing long notes that start with the kicks and hold until the next, on the root and the flat seventh, flat sixth or fifth. Add an EQ with a low shelf lift of about 3 dB at 50 Hz, and a Compressor at ratio 4:1.
4. Melody: an Instrument Track called Bells with Bell Pluck or Glass Pad, playing a short, dark 2-bar melody in the minor scale, repeated with a change at the end, sent to a Reverb Bus with a long reverb and the "Quarter echo" Delay preset.
5. Leave the melody out of the first 2 bars as an intro, and drop the drums for the last beat of bar 8 before the loop comes round.

Say the tempo, key, and what each Track plays.
